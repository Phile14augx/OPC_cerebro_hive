<architectural_fork_resolution>
01-RESEARCH.md flags a genuine fork: the schema has two parallel tenant-root conventions —
`Tenant -> Workspace -> Agent/Workflow/Project` (uses `workspaceId String @db.Uuid`) and
`Organization -> OrgMembership/Invitation/AIUsageRecord/Prompt` (uses `orgId String`).

Resolved by CONTEXT.md D-16 (locked): `Policy` follows the Organization pattern. This matches GOVN-01's "scoped per organization" wording. Do not use `workspaceId`, do not add a `Workspace` relation, do not add both.

Also from RESEARCH.md: `Metric`, `Alert` and `Incident` are themselves bare stubs with no tenant scoping — they are NOT a precedent to copy. The audit-field and index shape come from `Agent`; the FK type comes from `OrgMembership`.
</architectural_fork_resolution>
