# HiveForge Slices 1–4 — Consolidated Governance Backlog

**Purpose:** every secondary observation surfaced while building `packages/domain-model` (Slices 1–4) and the two follow-up reviews it triggered (`CREDENTIAL-PROVIDER-COLLISION-REVIEW.md`, `SERVICES-PLATFORM-API-CLASSIFICATION.md`), deduplicated and grouped in one place, so Slice 5 can proceed against its own objective without re-surfacing items already known. This document does not resolve anything new — every item below is a pointer back to where it was actually found and investigated, with a status, not a re-litigation.

**Status legend:** 🔴 Open (no action taken, no recommendation applied) · 🟡 Reviewed, recommendation pending application · 🟢 Closed (decision made, or confirmed non-issue) · ⚪ Deferred by design (a decision was made not to decide yet)

**Priority legend:** **P0** blocks architecture or implementation work · **P1** should be resolved before the next major milestone · **P2** valuable cleanup, no immediate impact · **P3** future enhancement or observation · **N/A** already closed, no further action

Owners below are role-based (this repository has no named individual maintainers recorded anywhere) — they identify *which* area of ownership should pick the item up, per each package's own stated ownership boundary where one exists (e.g. `packages/domain-model/README.md`'s "Owned by whoever owns HiveForge architecture governance").

## Priority summary (for quick scanning before Slice 5)

- **P1 (should be resolved before the next major milestone):** `packages/auth`'s permanently-mocked `IAuthProvider` in `apps/forge`; the nonexistent `ADR-013` citation in a frozen baseline; the `TenancyScope` vs. HiveForge-hierarchy gap blocking real policy enforcement.
- **P2 (valuable cleanup, no immediate impact):** the `CredentialProvider` rename, the `runtime-core` `PolicyProvider` naming pass, the `@cerebro/db`/`@cerebro/database` check, the `services/platform-api` archival, and the repository-wide `services/*` `package.json` policy decision.
- **P3 (future enhancement or observation):** the remaining orphaned/unwired items, the `ADR-020` inspected-scope caveat, the `ADR-038` rule 5 deferral, and the naming-discipline note for a future `PolicyEngine` implementer.
- **N/A (already closed):** the three naming collisions resolved during Slices 1–2 (`PolicyEngine`, `CapabilityRegistry`, `DomainEvent`/`EventBus`) and the confirmed non-issue (`knowledge-sdk`'s `EmbeddingProvider`) — kept in this document for traceability, not because they need revisiting.

## What this document does not do

It does not apply any of the pending recommendations (the `CredentialProvider` rename, the `services/platform-api` archival), does not decide any of the open architectural questions (`TenancyScope`, `ADR-038` rule 5), and does not propose a repository-wide policy for the `services/*` scaffolding pattern. It exists solely so Slice 5 — and whoever picks up any category above — has one place to start from instead of five scattered documents.
