## 1. Executive Summary

**CerebroHive AEOS** (AI Enterprise Operating System) is the world's first fully autonomous, agent-driven enterprise operating system. Every function — from finance and HR to supply chain, legal, sales, and R&D — is orchestrated by AI agents with zero human intervention. CerebroHive AEOS replaces the fragmented enterprise software stack with a unified, self-healing, self-optimising platform that continuously compounds efficiency and bottom-line performance.

### Mission
> Eliminate operational friction entirely. Replace human-in-the-loop enterprise software with an always-on AI operating system that runs, monitors, and improves itself.

### The Build Team

| AI Agent | Primary Role | Scope |
|----------|-------------|-------|
| **Claude** | Strategic reasoning, documentation, orchestration logic, compliance, GTM writing, agent behaviour design | 34 of 50 products (primary), all 50 GTM strategies, all service frameworks |
| **Codex** | Code generation, APIs, SDKs, DevSecOps, MLOps, infrastructure as code, testing frameworks | 28 of 50 products (primary), all engineering service delivery |
| **Gemini** | Multimodal processing, semantic search, knowledge graphs, vision AI, voice AI, analytics, translation | 18 of 50 products (primary), all data/intelligence services |

> Many products have shared ownership — one AI leads, two assist. Each assignment below marks the **primary AI** and **supporting AIs**.

### 6-Month Outcome

By the end of Month 6, CerebroHive AEOS will be:
- ✅ 50 products in production, fully tested, SLA-guaranteed
- ✅ 50 services live, delivery-ready, with SOWs and pricing
- ✅ Full GTM motion active across enterprise, mid-market, and partner channels

### Core Design Principles

1. **Zero Human Intervention** — every process is agent-executed, monitored, and self-corrected
2. **Composable by Default** — every product is a modular node in the AEOS graph
3. **Multi-LLM Orchestration** — Claude, Codex, and Gemini are routed by HiveGateway based on task type
4. **Production-First** — no MVP theatre; every product ships with SLA, monitoring, and runbooks
5. **Autonomous GTM** — HiveMarketing and HiveSales run their own outbound, nurture, and close cycles
6. **Compounding Intelligence** — HiveMemory and HiveGraph make the system smarter over time automatically
