## System of record (Milestone 25.4C conclusion)

Closest to **Option D (independent products)**, qualified: HiveSwarm and the Platform Runtime (`gateway`/`platform-api`/`forge-api`/Kotlin trio) show no confirmed network wiring to each other in either direction — no route, no env var, no client call, in any source or manifest read — while shipping through the same Helm chart, namespace, ArgoCD project, and CI pipeline. Whether they're meant to stay independent or are missing integration work is a product question, not something code alone resolves. Full evidence chain in `MILESTONE-25.4C-RUNTIME-INTEGRATION.md`.
