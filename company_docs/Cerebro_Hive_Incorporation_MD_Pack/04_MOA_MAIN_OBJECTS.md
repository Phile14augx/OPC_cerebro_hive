# 04 — MOA Main Objects — Working Draft

## Principal Business Description
Cerebro Hive is an enterprise artificial intelligence software company developing an **Enterprise AI Operating System** that enables organizations to build, deploy, orchestrate, automate, secure, govern, and operate AI agents, models, applications, enterprise data, and AI-powered business workflows.

## Proposed Main Object
> To carry on the business of designing, developing, researching, engineering, creating, owning, operating, hosting, licensing, implementing, integrating, maintaining, distributing and commercializing enterprise software, artificial intelligence platforms, software-as-a-service products, cloud computing technologies and related applications, systems and infrastructure, including artificial intelligence agents, autonomous and semi-autonomous agents, multi-agent systems, machine learning systems, generative artificial intelligence, large language model technologies, enterprise automation and workflow orchestration, data and knowledge management, retrieval-augmented generation, knowledge graphs, enterprise search and analytics, artificial intelligence model management and operations, artificial intelligence security, governance, monitoring, risk and compliance technologies, and to provide consulting, engineering, implementation, integration, managed, operational, training and support services in connection therewith.

## Supporting Areas
- AI agents and multi-agent orchestration
- Enterprise automation and integrations
- RAG, knowledge graphs, enterprise search and analytics
- CRM, HR, finance, ERP, research and operations applications
- AgentOps, MLOps and LLMOps
- AI security, identity, governance, compliance and zero trust
- AI strategy, engineering, implementation and managed services
